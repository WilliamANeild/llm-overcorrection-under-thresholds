# Which numbers the introduction carries

Written 2026-09-08. Every value traced to `results_FINAL.md` by line. Nothing here is
recomputed; this is a selection question, not an analysis.

## The constraint the corpus imposes

From `rules/03`: numbers live in the results beat, and **every one is chaperoned by a
referent and a comparison**. A bare percentage appears nowhere in either corpus. The
model sentence is Laban's: "an average performance of 65%, a 25-point drop from
single-turn performances of 90%." Fourteen of 23 introductions state at least one number;
none reads as a results table.

The abstract is final and carries three percentages. The introduction opening on a
different set is the problem this list exists to close.

## Recommended set: four figures

Items 1, 2, 3 and 4, which is exactly the abstract's set plus the blind-reader null. That
puts the paper's two front sections on one story, and four figures sits inside corpus
practice for a results beat.

Items 5, 6 and 7 stay in the introduction as clauses without numbers. Nothing is dropped
from the argument; three things stop carrying digits.
